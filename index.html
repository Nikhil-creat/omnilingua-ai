Features

Everything a modern translator needs

Built like a product: fast, private, accessible and extensible.

🧠

Agentic pipeline

Analyze → Draft → Critique → Refine → Verify. Domain, register and idioms handled before translating.

🔌

Any AI provider

Paste a key: Groq, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, OpenRouter or xAI is auto-detected. Vault and automatic failover.

🛰

Edge AI mode

On-device translation with the browser's built-in model. No key, no data leaves your device.

📚

RAG translation memory

Upload glossaries (.json .tmx .csv). N-gram + optional neural search enforces your terminology.

📷

OCR & AI Vision

Scan images, camera shots and PDFs in 25 languages. Vision models read handwriting and complex scripts.

🗣

Voice interpreter

Face-to-face two-way interpreter, live interpreter mode and big-text live captions.

🧪

Quality audit

MQM scoring with error list, one-tap fixes, back-translation and glossary compliance check.

🛡

Privacy Shield

Masks emails, phones, Aadhaar/PAN, cards and IPs before text leaves your browser.

🔐

Encrypted vault

AES-GCM key storage with optional passphrase lock (PBKDF2), lock-now and idle auto-lock.

📦

Batch & Arena

Translate CSV/TXT/JSON in bulk, or pit two models against each other with an AI judge.

📊

Analytics & audit log

Latency, success rate, tokens, languages and models, with CSV export.

📲

Installable PWA

Works offline as an app shell, accepts shared text from other apps, with home-screen shortcuts.

How it works

From input to insight in four steps

1

Capture

Type, paste, speak, scan an image, or load a PDF, .srt or CSV.

2

Protect & enrich

Privacy Shield masks identifiers. RAG injects your mandatory terminology.

3

Agent pipeline

Analyze, draft, critique, refine and verify, in the cloud or fully on-device.

4

Deliver & learn

Speak, export, audit quality, and save approved terms back to memory.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it free?

The app is free and open source (MIT). You bring your own API key. Groq offers a free tier, and Edge mode needs no key at all.

Where is my API key stored?

Only in your browser, encrypted with AES-GCM. You can add a passphrase lock (PBKDF2). The key is sent only to the provider you choose.

Does it work offline?

The app shell loads offline. Cloud translation needs internet. Edge mode translates on-device where your browser supports the Translator API.

Which languages are supported?

25 including English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Urdu, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and Arabic.

How accurate are the translations?

AI can make mistakes. Use Deep mode, the MQM Quality audit and your glossary, and have critical legal or medical text reviewed by a human.

Which browser should I use?

Chrome or Edge give the best experience (voice, captions, Edge AI). Safari works partly. Firefox lacks speech recognition.

Can I self-host it?

Yes: GitHub Pages as-is, or Docker with Nginx using the included Dockerfile and compose file.
